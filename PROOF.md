## What we are not saying

- We do not know whether teams will pay. There have been no customer calls.
- Most evidence comes from Hacker News comments and GitHub issues from technical founders. That is not a representative sample of marketing teams.
- We have not measured whether a receipted memo changes conversion, reply rate, or retention.
- The 2-channel diversity is a weakness, not a badge. It is why the evidence dimension does not have a perfect score.
