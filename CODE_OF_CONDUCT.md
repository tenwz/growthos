# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want growthOS to be a place where people can disagree about marketing, evidence, and software without being made to feel small.

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or whether they think "growth" is a serious discipline.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the claim, the code, or the evidence — not the person.
- Assume competence. Ask why before concluding someone was careless.
- Be explicit about uncertainty. "I don't know" is a complete answer.
- Credit prior work, including competitors.
- Keep examples free of real customer names and private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private customer data or credentials.
- Spam, mass unsolicited outreach, or scraping in the name of growth.
- Bad-faith arguments about whether a rule should be enforceable.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via GitHub or the contact in the repository profile. Reports are handled privately. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.
