# What the market is telling us — Week 45

## Executive summary

Summit Air owners do not want another analytics dashboard. The repeated pain is revenue leaking through missed calls, no-shows, and unsent replacement quotes. They want recovery, not reporting.

## Claims

- **Owner-operated HVAC shops lose 10–15 calls a week, and those callers often buy from the next company.** [@sig_01M6MBR84KRGF61N8MSPXGR900] [@sig_01M6MBR84TRBD1JXP1SQ43E9SC] [@sig_01M6MBR84W8DKZDPDCHRZ4PC59]

- **Price sensitivity drops when ROI is framed as recovered jobs: "If you show me eight recovered jobs a month, I do not care what the software costs."** [@sig_01M6MBR84RT5WR09WTYMZ3KDPS] [@sig_01M6MBR84V8ZXYFZY917FSD6TR] [@sig_01M6MBR84WM5M1EMP3SYAP7JKX]
- **After-hours calls are a separate wedge: "They will pay for speed."** [@sig_01M6MBR84R5C23045ABX0VET25] [@sig_01M6MBR84X6GT04M6MQM6CAD1R] [@sig_01M6MBR84YBZR6V2FB0KEYGKK6]
- **Existing field-service tools report missed calls but do not run recovery: "it is a scoreboard, not a fix."** [@sig_01M6MBR84R3F2DWXY04089NXZ0] [@sig_01M6MBR84VJDDD1MR5J7QFEXWD] [@sig_01M6MBR84ZC4BZJA6GQND5VTTT]

## What we are not saying

- We are not saying owners want a fully autonomous voice agent. The evidence says the opposite: they want a human-sounding callback and a human to close the job.
- We are not saying this generalizes to national roll-ups with 24/7 call centers. We have zero signals from that segment.
- We are not saying price is irrelevant. We are saying buyers compare recovered jobs, not software seats.

## What to test next

Test a missed-call recovery landing page against a "no-show calculator" lead magnet. The primary metric is qualified demo requests. [@exp_01M6MBR852Q2ZKTT18Z2P29C1C]
