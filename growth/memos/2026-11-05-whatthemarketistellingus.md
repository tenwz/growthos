# What the market is telling us — week of Nov 5

## Executive summary

The market does not need more marketing generation. It needs a way to tell which generated claim is true, which channel is still working, and which experiment actually closed. The same week, the largest marketing-skills repo shipped security and routing fixes, a spec-driven CLI founder said enforcement is the moat, and a GEO linter founder drew the same line: deterministic rules, creative agent.

## Claims

1. **AI outbound is saturating itself.** When every competitor runs an AI SDR, the channel collapses; the remaining differentiator is proof of work and reputation. [@sig_01M6MC5ASE1WFDVMQGNMHCHPK1] [@sig_01M6MC5KK6K8MB8J7M70Z66KAC]
2. **Content-led distribution is eroding.** A founder reports SEO article traffic declining "over the last year despite huge efforts," another says AI overviews end ~60% of searches without a click, and a 2023 comment predicted this exact shift. [@sig_01M6MC5ASJV4KNN2P5T2Q33T5A] [@sig_01M6MC5ASJ1F0A5TNT8DZW31FX] [@sig_01M6MC5ASKJDVHKZ5D7XC580HY]
3. **AI-generated marketing is now a trust liability.** Readers describe "AI slop" fatigue, say AI-written articles "sound the same," and bounce from "vibe generated website content fatigue." [@sig_01M6MC5ASKW92YAHXCFTG08MK3] [@sig_01M6MC5ASM2QXQ9DP7SD1WBZEF] [@sig_01M6MC5ASMERS5J0XNXWFYHRVP]
4. **AI visibility is an opaque second search channel.** 94.8% of eligible sites are never cited, strong Google SEO does not predict AI visibility, and an audit of 98,020 claims found ~11% unsupported by the cited page. [@sig_01M6MC5ASNXA2JSTJ7V0V0WN5S] [@sig_01M6MC5ASN6MSK7XBWNX5X38A7] [@sig_01M6MC5KJYAYSNJV2QH4AMYA0N]
5. **Attribution is fuzzy enough that operators fall back on consistency and direct contact.** Two practitioners call attribution "the bane" and "notoriously fuzzy"; a former head of growth says "90% of marketing is consistency, not brilliance." [@sig_01M6MC5KK2FDV1J2QBK60QV178] [@sig_01M6MC5KK34DZ15Q2B27GGF6A9] [@sig_01M6MC5KK3H7Z6FEHB1FH1S7RA]
6. **Builders want enforcement, not another prompt.** A spec-driven CLI founder says "spec enforcement is where the value is: checking a diff against the spec and failing loudly"; a GEO linter founder says "the linter is deterministic, the AI agent is creative"; and a 145-skill install cut the right-skill pick rate from 21/28 to 14/28. [@sig_01M6MC5KK40Q9X8NGFRY1Y8MMX] [@sig_01M6MC5KK4NPF95MMJ7BKYKEE7] [@sig_01M6MC5KK4H9SAFQ8VA9Y5AQRY]

## What we are not saying

- We do not have evidence that teams will pay for this. There have been zero design-partner calls so far.
- Most signals are public Hacker News comments and GitHub issues from technical founders. They are not a representative sample of marketing teams.
- We have not measured whether a receipted memo changes conversion, reply rate, or retention.

## Counterevidence

- SpecPilot launched on Product Hunt with 4 votes and about 100 visitors. Enforcement tooling does not solve distribution. [@sig_01M6MC5KK40Q9X8NGFRY1Y8MMX]
- The skills layer is moving fast: a competing pack is actively adding honest visual workflows and security checks. A static prompt pack could catch up on features, even if not on evidence. [@sig_01M6MC5KK5Q8F3YK0B5T2C3FKC] [@sig_01M6MC5KK5HATVADTAYBFMMD0Q]
