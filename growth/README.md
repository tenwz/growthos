# growthOS — the repo's own Growth OS

This is not a demo. It is the actual growth memory for growthOS, maintained in public.

## Rules

1. Signals are real, public, and quoted verbatim with a URL.
2. No synthetic signals here. Synthetic data lives in `examples/hvac-saas`.
3. Memos are drafted weekly and published only after a human review.
4. Experiments end in a decision and a learning, including failures.
5. Private customer calls use `provenance: internal` and `consent: anonymized`; names are removed.
6. Nothing here is edited after publication. Corrections supersede.

## Layout

```text
signals/      captured public signals
memos/        weekly memos with receipts
experiments/  launch experiments and their ledger
learnings/    written learnings from closed experiments
agents/       the six job specs
metrics/      weekly public metrics (stars, installs, calls)
cockpit/      generated static dashboard and badge
content/      founder-content drafts
outbound/     outbound drafts; a human sends
ai-search/    AI visibility probes and gaps
```
