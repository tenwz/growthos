# Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary segment

- **Role:** technical founder, head of growth, or marketing lead who can read a diff.
- **Company size:** 2–50 people, pre-seed to Series A, software or dev tools.
- **Industry:** developer tools, AI products, B2B SaaS, agencies serving those companies.
- **Trigger events:** launch, first marketing hire, AI content embarrassment, a channel suddenly stops working, board asks for attribution.
- **Where they hang out:** Hacker News, X, GitHub, Claude Code / Cursor communities, small founder Slack groups.
- **What they read:** technical postmortems, launch retrospectives, honest growth numbers.

## Buying committee

| Person | Cares about | Objection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical founder | Speed, control, no SaaS | "I can build this myself" |
| Marketing lead | Proof, attribution, fewer dumb claims | "My team won't maintain Markdown" |
| Engineer | Determinism, CI, no lock-in | "Another tool to install" |

## Anti-ICP

- Enterprise marketing orgs that need SSO, approval workflows, and a hosted UI on day one.
- Teams that want fully automated outreach with no human review.
- Agencies that sell content volume, not evidence.
- Anyone who will not commit quotes and URLs.

## Qualification signal

They already keep customer notes somewhere. The question is not "do you want memory?" but "can your agent read it?"
