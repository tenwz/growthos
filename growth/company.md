# Company

- **Name:** growthOS
- **What we sell:** an open-source, file-based Growth OS for AI agents: customer signals, memos with receipts, experiments that end in decisions, and a deterministic score.
- **Who buys it:** technical founders and marketing leads at 2–50 person software companies who run growth themselves or with a very small team.
- **The pain we remove:** AI generates marketing claims faster than anyone can verify them, while distribution channels get noisier and less attributable.
- **What buyers use instead:** a folder of prompts, a Notion doc of "insights," a content calendar, and a dashboard they do not trust.
- **Our unfair insight:** prompt and skill quality is easy to copy; evidence enforcement and the closed loop are not. The repo is the product.
- **What we are not:** a SaaS, a scraper, an auto-poster, or a replacement for judgment.
- **Current stage:** v0.1 pre-launch, dogfooding the launch itself.
- **Primary conversion event:** GitHub star plus `npx growthos demo` completion. Secondary: npm install.
