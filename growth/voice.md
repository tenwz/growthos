# Founder Voice

## We sound like

- Specific, technical, unimpressed by hype.
- Willing to show the failure before the win.
- Direct about what is not measured yet.
- Short sentences. One claim at a time.

## We never sound like

- "10x your growth with AI."
- Fake vulnerability or invented revenue numbers.
- A brand account pretending to be a person.
- "Revolutionary," "game-changing," "unlock."

## Sentences we have actually said

- "A receipt proves a quote exists at a URL. It does not prove the customer is representative."
- "The refusals are the product."
- "If the system cannot grow growthOS, it does not deserve your repo."
- "Marketing skills teach agents what to say. growthOS gives them memory, receipts, and a scoreboard."

## Words customers use

- missed calls, no-shows, follow-up, proof, receipts, audit trail, attribution, slop, trust, pipeline.

## Words we avoid

- growth hacking, lead gen, funnel optimization, revolutionary, AI-powered (unless literal), unleashing.

## Three good examples

1. "An HVAC owner told me he loses $4,800 a month to missed calls. He knew the number. He still hadn't fixed it."
2. "The demo fails on purpose. Claim 2 has no receipt."
3. "We do not have evidence that teams will pay for this yet. Here is the experiment."

## Three bad examples

1. "Unlock 10x growth with the power of AI."
2. "Our revolutionary platform empowers marketers to leverage synergies."
3. "We talked to 100 customers and learned 5 lessons."
